# Dashboard Story

## 1. City Ops Lead

### Headline
Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate and should be the first city targeted for operational improvement.

### Evidence
Bengaluru recorded 17 SLA breaches across 107 bookings, giving it an SLA breach rate of 15.89%. This is higher than the overall dashboard SLA breach rate of 13.17% (79 breaches out of 600 bookings). Hyderabad is the next-highest city at 14.29%.

### Implication
City Ops should prioritize Bengaluru for root-cause analysis and service-level improvements. Reducing breaches in Bengaluru would have a meaningful impact on the overall SLA performance, while Hyderabad should also be monitored as the next-highest city.

## 2. Category Lead

### Headline
Deep Home Cleaning is the strongest category, while Salon for Women is comparatively under-booked.

### Evidence
Deep Home Cleaning generated ₹508,964 in revenue from 176 bookings, making it the strongest category by both revenue and booking volume. In comparison, Salon for Women had only 65 bookings and generated ₹151,689 in revenue. The dashboard total across all categories is ₹1,047,973 from 600 bookings.

### Implication
The Category team should continue protecting the strong performance of Deep Home Cleaning while investigating the lower booking volume for Salon for Women. Increasing demand and bookings for Salon for Women could provide an opportunity to improve category-level contribution without relying only on the strongest category.
